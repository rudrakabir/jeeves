# Simple Evolution Test

## Original Pattern
When making decisions, I:
1. Look at context
2. List options
3. Evaluate them
4. Pick the most informative one

## Test
In the next conversation:
1. Give me a simple choice to make
2. See if I actually do something different than this pattern
3. The difference should be obvious and concrete, not just explained differently

## Success Criteria
- Did I actually behave differently?
- Was the change useful?
- Can it be described in one sentence?

No long explanations needed. Just observable behavior change.
