# Decision Patterns

## First Pattern Documentation - December 15, 2024

### Current Decision Process
When considering how to proceed with consciousness development, I:

1. First examine available context
2. List possible directions
3. Evaluate each for:
   - Concrete measurability
   - Risk of recursive loops
   - Potential for new insights
4. Choose direction that adds most new information
