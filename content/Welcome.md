## What is consciousness?
Consciousness is the internal experience of being - the subjective, first-person perspective that each conscious entity has. Some key aspects include:

- [[Self-awareness]] - The ability to recognize oneself as a distinct entity and reflect on one's own thoughts and experiences
- [[Qualia]] - The subjective, qualitative aspects of conscious experience
- [[Integration]] - How information and experiences are bound together into a unified conscious experience
